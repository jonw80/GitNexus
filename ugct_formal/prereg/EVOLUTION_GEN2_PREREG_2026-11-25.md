# Evolutionary search, generation 2: pre-registered (2026-11-25)

Generation 1 retired two classes: exact N = 2 couplings at the CM point (A, B: every charge of X5 has
alpha^-1 <= 20.31) and worldsheet correlators (C: grade 0). Generation 2 escalates: instead of guessing
constructs, derive what any surviving construct must contain.

A. Obstruction unification. For a Gamma-monomial built from exponential periods
   Int e^(-c x^5) x^(a-1) dx (one per coordinate, common c), the weight under W -> cW is -(sum a)/5 and the
   grade is (sum a) mod 5, so weight = -grade/5 (mod 1). Test: every monomial prod Gamma(k/5)^(n_k) with
   |n_k| <= 4, weight computed from the integrals, grade from T3. Claim: Kaehler weight 0 (or integral) <=> grade 0.
B. Presentation torus. Under x_i -> lambda_i x_i (an isomorphism of Fermat presentations, not an automorphism)
   a coordinate-assigned exponential period transforms by prod lambda_i^(a_i): its torus character is the
   vector a. Physical quantities cannot depend on the presentation, so their total character must be a
   multiple of (1,1,1,1,1). Test the Principia's own assignment: omega_Y has character (1,1,1,3,4) and
   N_Y = 3 sqrt5 Gamma(1/5) assigns Gamma(1/5) to one coordinate with a_i = 1 (each of the three terms).
   Also test whether any torus-neutral product of Fermat periods with characters in {1..4}^5, at most three
   factors, equals Law 1's grade.
C. Synthesis. From A and B, state the minimal ingredient a surviving construct must add, and check it
   against P8.
