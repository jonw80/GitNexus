## G4-1 Universal sigma-grade

**Claim.** Define sigma(beta pi^s prod Gamma(a)^(n_a)) = s + sum n_a a (mod 1), where beta is algebraic, s is in (1/2)Z and a is rational. Then:
- (i) sigma is unchanged by translation, reflection and Gauss multiplication, the relations of the Lang-Rohrlich conjecture, with sqrt(pi) = Gamma(1/2) and so sigma(pi^s) = s.
- (ii) sigma = 0 on every Beta value and every Fermat period of every degree N, in any number of variables.
- (iii) sigma(Law 1) = 1/5.

Conditional on K2 (Lang-Rohrlich at all denominators), Law 1 therefore lies outside the field generated by pi, Qbar and all Fermat and CM periods of every degree. Under the Fresan-Jossen exponential-period conjecture it is not a period of any variety. The real structure and the duplication formula (tenths) do not escape this, since sigma(Gamma(1/10) Gamma(3/5)/sqrt(pi)) = 1/5.

**Test.**
- Use 20,000 random instances of the three relation families, with denominators up to 60 and m up to 12. Check each numerically to 40 digits and check sigma(LHS) = sigma(RHS) from the structured terms.
- Compute sigma of Law 1 and of the Principia's other Gamma-laws (sin^2 theta_W, lambda_H, v_0, the V_cb factor).
- The pass/fail logic gets a unit test, with mutated relations that must fail.

**Predicted verdict.**
- As a no-go: Accepted, conditional on K2.
- As a closure: Critical, since it supplies no grade-1 object.

## G4-2 Integral thimble lattice of the four-field model

Arithmetic transplant; never applied.

**Claim.**
- The rapid-decay lattice of W_4 = x_1^5 + ... + x_4^5 is spanned by products of thimbles. Its periods against x_4^2 d^4x (character (1,1,1,3)) fill C (1 - zeta)^4 Z[zeta_5], with C = Gamma(1/5)^3 Gamma(3/5)/5^4.
- (i) Law 1 is not |Z(gamma)| for any integral gamma. That would need |m|^2 = T^2 = (390625 + 171875 sqrt5)/8 with m in Z[zeta_5], but T^2 is not an algebraic integer. The prime 2 is inert in Q(zeta_5), so it is the obstruction.
- (ii) 2 Law 1 = |Z(gamma*)| for m* = 125 phi^2 (1 - zeta^2). So 2 is the smallest integral multiple.
- (iii) Rescaling the form by an integer n cannot help, because T^2/n^2 stays non-integral at 2.

**Test.** Exact arithmetic in Q(zeta_20) with exact_forms.F: the characteristic polynomial of T^2 and of (2T)^2, and the identity |m*|^2 = (2T)^2. Then a 50-digit check of 2 Law 1 = C |1 - zeta|^4 |m*|.

**Pass.** Accept only if Law 1 itself is an integral central charge.

**Predicted verdict.** Critical. Law 1 is half an integral BPS mass, not an integral one.

## G4-3 Fractional-dimension Feynman integrals

A non-standard regularization that breaks integer spacetime dimension; never applied.

**Claim to test.** Consider the canonical massless family of nested one-loop insertions,

where G(a,b;d) = Gamma(a+b-d/2) Gamma(d/2-a) Gamma(d/2-b)/(Gamma(a) Gamma(b) Gamma(d-a-b)) is the Chetyrkin-Tkachov function.

Take d = 4 - 2k/5 (k = 1..9) and L = 1..6. Compute the Gamma-content of I_L modulo Qbar^x pi^(Z/2), and count the (d, L) whose content is Law 1's, (3, -1) on (Gamma(1/5), Gamma(2/5)). Also record sigma(I_L) = L k/5 (mod 1).

**Predicted verdict.** Critical: either no match, or matches found only by scanning.

## E2 Period-ring triage (meta-technique)

Instead of building constructs, classify each mechanism class by the ring its outputs lie in. That ring is one of: Qbar, the periods P, or the exponential periods EP.

Every class with outputs in P is retired at once, given G4-1 and K2. This covers:
- critical Calabi-Yau periods and D-brane central charges;
- lattice minima and couplings;
- Lyapunov exponents of algebraic matrices (logarithms of algebraic numbers);
- L-values (periods by the Beilinson-Deligne conjectures);
- heights and regulators;
- Feynman integrals in integer dimension at rational kinematics (Bogner-Weinzierl);
- multiple zeta values.

The survivors are mechanisms whose outputs are exponential periods with a physical scale (R3), or Gamma-functions from non-integer exponents (G4-3).

**Test.** The sigma-screen of G4-1 is applied to every quantity computed in generations 1-3 and to the Principia's Gamma-laws.
